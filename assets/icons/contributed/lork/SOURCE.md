# lork's icons

`adjustments.svg`, `selection.svg`, `brush.svg` and `transform.svg` are by lork
(https://github.com/lork-fork), drawn from basic shapes in a vector editor and given in
https://github.com/artcraft-android/procraft/pull/178. They are kept as given;
`tools/icons/draw_icons.py` rounds them and places them in the bar, and shortens the Transform
arrow's head and draws out its tail. Licence: MIT OR Apache-2.0, as the rest of ProCraft.
